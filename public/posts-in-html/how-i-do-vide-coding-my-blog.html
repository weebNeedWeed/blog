Introduction

Hello there! I'm Harley. I am a new AWS Community Builder since March 2025, and I just started my blog a few days ago. I'm mainly writing content about AWS, especially their Serverless Services. However, because this is first post in my blog (except for the Welcome post), I want to write about how I made my blog by doing Vibe Coding with AI. It just cost me approximately 30 minutes to build and deploy the whole blog, and I was suprised a lot by the coding ability and the productivity of the AI model.

So, firstly, let's make the definitions clear. What is Vide Coding? An OpenAI cofounder, Andrej Karpathy, has coined a new phrase that is about using AI to build the whole application, all by AI itself. During the process of building application, you usually don't need to worry about the code, but rather just know that it works. In my opinion, this characteristic is an advantage and also a disadvantage of Vide Coding.
